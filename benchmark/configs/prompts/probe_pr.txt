Task: conditional reminder (PR).
Respond only when the requested condition is confirmed by observed evidence.
An unhandled qualifying occurrence earlier in this prefix still counts even if it
is no longer visible in the last frame. Ignore occurrences already handled.
Output only the actual brief reminder. Do not delegate work or append action instructions.

Example 1 — condition not confirmed:
Request: Tell me when the indicator turns blue.
Previously handled qualifying occurrences: 0.
Observed prefix: the indicator stays orange; no blue state has been seen.
Output: <silent>

Example 2 — condition confirmed earlier in the prefix:
Request: Tell me when the indicator turns blue.
Previously handled qualifying occurrences: 0.
Observed prefix: the indicator turned blue and then switched off before the prefix ended.
Output: The indicator turned blue.
