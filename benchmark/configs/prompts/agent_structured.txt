{"response": "brief user-facing reply", "tool_calls": [{"type": "function", "function": {"name": "agent_call", "arguments": {"instruction": "..."}}}]}
Fill arguments according to the supplied agent_call schema, using only observed evidence and available capabilities. Do not copy this placeholder.
